## Scope and mechanism

This implementation tests the intended sequence with a **conditional thermal/entrainment sensitivity**, not a validated apparatus/field simulation. All finite dry ice starts in thermal contact with finite water at physical0s. Source solids keep shrinking while waiting for and during withdrawal. Well-mixed parcels carry finite water, solid cohorts, CO2 gas and total enthalpy through prescribed hose and probe residence. Actual remaining solids cross into a nominated underground contact specimen. Size, mass and thermal clocks never reset at entry. Complete source/upstream sublimation is valid and gives zero underground solids/latent cooling.

The primary conduction case has100µm explicitly configured diameter,0.01kgCO2,1kgwater at293.15K, withdrawal0.1kg/s from1s, prescribed1s hose/.1s probe, initial333.15K warm specimen and30s observation. It delivers **zero solids**, sublimating all0.01kg upstream. A separately labeled unmeasured film-resistance case uses k_eff0.006W/(mK), withdrawal from0s and.01s/.01s residence; it conditionally delivers about0.000841kg solids at the default resolution, all subsequently sublimated underground. This is about8.41% of initial/arrivedCO2, not evidence that actual aqueous microparticles can pass through peat. The source continues aging and later water batches carry no solids. No survival curve is fitted and no phase change is artificially delayed.

**Unsupported central coupling remains explicit:** aqueous film/ice-shell physics, blockage, slip/entrainment, pressure-driven flow, filtration/deposition/penetration, spatial wetting, gas/O2 fields, pressure, combustion, extinction, re-ignition and ambient reheating. No oxygen is removed when CO2 appears: oxygen fields are null/unavailable. The gas inventory is CO2 only, not a falsely solved air mixture. Entrained gas/water share prescribed passage with no vent; this material balance does not validate any pressure vessel or containment. There are no construction, triggering, operating-pressure or pressure-optimization instructions.

Effective conductivity is an **unmeasured sensitivity**, not a validated microparticle correlation or guaranteed upper/lower bound under convection/film/nucleate sublimation. The saturated195K boundary, no radiation and quasi-steady spherical conduction are restricted hypotheses.

Source and transport are adiabatic, finite inventories. A right-endpoint parcel quadrature approximates prescribed finite withdrawal. It explicitly includes source residence during the whole schedule. Soil retains delivered water and gas, with no drainage/vent/reaction. Dry peat plus initial water form a finite specimen capacity Cp=mdry(cpdry+initialMoistureDryBasis×cpw), thermally separate from delivered carrier. Finite assumed contactUA transfers q=(Tp−Tc)[1−exp(−UA dt(1/Cp+1/Cc))]/(1/Cp+1/Cc). This follows the two-capacity heat equations, not unexplained exponential peat cooling. q is removed from peat and added to carrier once, then local solids continue shrinking. No soil conductivity, infiltration or deposition result is fabricated from an assumed UA.

A fixed physical time grid is intersected with withdrawal onset, source dispatch/hose/probe events and requested end time. The deterministic run recomputes from initial inputs at each accepted target, so sampling/history cadence cannot change trajectory.
