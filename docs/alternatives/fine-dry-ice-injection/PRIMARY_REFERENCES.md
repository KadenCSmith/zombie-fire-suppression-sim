# Agent D primary evidence notes (pre-implementation)

Read2026-10-12; no empirical delivery/suppression results supplied by user.

- NIST Chemistry WebBook SRD69, CO2 phase-change data: https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C124389&Mask=24 . Giauque & Egan1937 value25.2kJ/mol at195K => L=25200/0.0440095=572603.6J/kg. The listed fit spans154–196K. It does not validate aqueous microparticle heat transfer or a pressurized mixture. NIST Antoine relation log10(P/bar)=6.81228-1301.679/(T/K-3.494),154.26–195.89K, is a saturated pure-CO2 reference only. No vessel pressure calculation is proposed.
- IAPWS SR6-08(2011), liquid water near0.1MPa: https://iapws.org/technical-guidance/release/LiquidWater . Correlations include heat capacity and conductivity; conductivity lower limit273.15K. Approximate constant cp4186J/(kgK) and k0.6W/(mK) would be explicitly labeled numerical-study approximations around room temperature, not a full IAPWS implementation or cryogenic-water property extrapolation.
- Aoki, Sawada & Akahori2002, International Journal of Refrigeration25,235–242, https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-7007(01)00084-6 ; readable publisher abstract/conclusions https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0140700701000846 . Actual dry-ice/water direct-contact experiments and a vapor/water two-layer freezing analysis; film and nucleate regimes observed. The accessible experimental sample is45mm diameter,90mm length. These results establish that freezing and vapor-film resistance require attention; they do not supply a correlation validated for10–500µm particles in hoses/peat. Full local ice-shell/blockage coupling remains unavailable.
- Purandare, Verbruggen & Vanapalli2023, International Communications in Heat and Mass Transfer148,107042, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icheatmasstransfer.2023.107042 ; open primary PDF https://ris.utwente.nl/ws/files/320947389/1-s2.0-S0735193323004311-main.pdf . Measured dry-ice sphere surface temperatures depend on ambient pressure and CO2 concentration. Near195K applies to saturated CO2 surroundings, not arbitrary unsaturated air. A fixed195K surface therefore stays an expressly restricted saturated-film assumption, not universal physics.
- Dissanayaka etal2012, Vadose Zone Journal, https://doi.org/10.2136/vzj2011.0092 . Measured peaty-soil thermal properties vary with water/solid fractions and shrinkage. Without peat-specific measurements/geometry, a single assumed specimen heat capacity/UA is an unvalidated calorimeter sensitivity and cannot predict field wetting or suppression.
- Heat Capacity of Drained Peat Soils2022, Applied Sciences12,1579, https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/12/3/1579 . Primary measurements on peat material; does not validate elevated-temperature reacting peat, particle filtration, or the user apparatus.

Derived rather than fitted: steady spherical Laplace conduction T(s)=Tfar-(Tfar-Ts)*r/s gives Qdot=4πkr(Tfar-Ts). Combining with d(4πρr³/3)/dt=-Qdot/L gives r² decay for a fixed bath. This is an analytical reference; an explicit effective conductivity used for film-resistance sensitivity has no empirical validation and is not a guaranteed bound under convection/nucleate sublimation.

Density1560kg/m³, gas cp850J/(kgK), peat cp1500J/(kgK), any particle size, residence, UA and contact geometry are candidate study assumptions requiring explicit provenance/uncertainty; do not call them measured. NIST gas Shomate coefficients begin298K and must not be extrapolated down195K silently.
