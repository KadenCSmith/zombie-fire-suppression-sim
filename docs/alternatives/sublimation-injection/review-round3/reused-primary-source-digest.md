- [NIST water phase data](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C7732185&Mask=4), SRD 69: tabulated normal boiling temperature 373.17 ± .04 K is consistent with the inherited fixed 373.15 K plateau as an approximation. Pressure variation is not solved by this proxy.

The solver instead uses constant 4186 J/kg/K; the citation cannot establish broad-range property accuracy or extrapolation validity.

The actual proxy does not evaluate this formulation; its presence in references earns no implementation credit.

(2021)](https://doi.org/10.1071/WF20117), International Journal of Wildland Fire 30(5), 378–387, publisher page: laboratory suppression results distinguish uniform penetration from channeling and discuss runoff/contact effects. Its peat-specific findings are not matched calibration or held-out validation of this module and are not transplanted as treatment parameters.
