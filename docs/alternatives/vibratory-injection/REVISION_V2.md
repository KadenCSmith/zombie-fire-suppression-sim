## Experimental source implementation

`field.ts` owns a new instance of the existing unchanged `CoupledTransport`. It uses the conservative fixed32×32×20 material atlas constructor, projected to16×8×10 cells over8×8×3.2m. This preserves prepared initial extensive fuel/water/energy inventories across resolution choices. The inherited dry preparation halo and assumed hot region are not an experimental ignition dataset. No CO₂/dry ice, heater, mechanics, fracture or cap source is enabled.

The driver requests≤0.125s physical steps by default. For each accepted internal step, it adds `dm=min(remaining,flow*acceptedDt)` to **total water**, using normalized trilinear weights around the prescribed advancing tip. It adds inlet enthalpy once: `dm*(4186*(Tin−273.15)+p_interface/1000)`J, assuming local absolute gas pressure equals interface pressure. Liquid density1000kg/m³ and constant heat capacity4186J/(kg K) are inherited approximations. A liquid inlet is rejected when its saturation pressure exceeds the assumed interface pressure. Phase resolution must retain finite positive gas pore space and nonnegative total water.

This is an accepted-step **post-step source split**, followed by equilibrium and ledger update. The outer transaction checkpoints transport arrays/ledgers, source budget, imported enthalpy/flow work, added-water support, tip and reaction rate. Any source failure restores the whole candidate, including partially resolved cells. No baseline is reset. Raw mass/energy/species residuals remain accessible separately from source-corrected residuals: mass−deliveredKg, thermal-ledger energy−importedEnthalpy, and water-species[3]−deliveredKg/MOLAR[3]. Other species retain their raw residuals. Chemical `totalEnergy()` is not mixed into the thermal/reaction ledger convention.

Step boundaries include finite supply exhaustion, cell faces and interpolation-support changes as the prescribed tip advances. Internal accepted steps can be shorter than requested; doses always use the returned accepted duration. There is no external unbounded retry loop; invalid source states fail with the last accepted snapshot. The constructor ceiling is at least0.1s to honor the unchanged scenario schema; the private driver still requests smaller steps for refinement.

The inherited model computes conductive heat exchange, gas Darcy/diffusive transport, gas/water phase equilibrium and one-step fuel oxidation with oxygen/temperature/moisture dependence. It does **not** compute liquid migration, lateral jets, pump/hose/gravity power, drilling forces, vibration energy, changing permeability, compaction/loosening, or root/soil deformation. The inherited ideal-gas, Darcy, constant-caloric and single-reaction assumptions limit physical validity. No sustained suppression/re-ignition threshold or treatment ranking is asserted.
