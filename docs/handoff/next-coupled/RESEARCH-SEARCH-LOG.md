# Research coverage and next search

Access date: 27 September 2026. This handoff used a targeted search of primary publisher pages and author/institutional repositories, plus the existing source-code bibliography. It is not an exhaustive systematic review of all research databases. No original machine-readable experimental dataset was downloaded, and no messages requesting unpublished data were sent.

## Work completed

Two research agents checked bibliographic identity, relevant scope, current/proposed code use and access depth. The source inventory was cross-checked against `src/sim/materialEvidence.ts` and `docs/COUPLED_MODEL.md`. Discovery included DOI/title lookups and topic searches for peat hydraulic properties, freezing/thawing, smolder spread, suppression and relevant thermophysical/mechanics methods.

Primary services reached included publisher sites for AIP/NIST, Wiley/AGU, CSIRO/ConnectSci, Elsevier/ScienceDirect, Frontiers, Copernicus, Springer and Emerald. Institutional/author records included NOAA, USGS, PolyU, Imperial, UCD, UPC, Illinois and Universitas Indonesia where accessible. Successful access varied; each bibliography entry states what was actually inspected. Search-index excerpts do not count as inspection of an unseen complete article.

The main new paired observations came from Schwärzel et al. (2006), Liu et al. (2022), Huang and Rein (2017), and Prat-Guitart et al. (2016). Their specimen conditions and exact table/section locations are recorded in the evidence register. They are distinct benchmark candidates and were not pooled or installed as one material.

Additional candidate literature screened during discovery included Päivänen (1973), Kechavarzi et al. (2010), Wang et al. (2021), Boelter (1969), and work on moisture-gradient propagation. Their numerical values were not promoted into this register because access or referee/data verification was incomplete. This screening is not a claim that those studies are unsuitable.

## Required continuation

Search accessible scholarly indexes and primary repositories more systematically, keeping exact query strings, dates, inclusion/exclusion reasons and article/data access status. Crossref, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar can aid discovery; peer-reviewed full papers and original datasets establish empirical evidence. Retrieve supplementary data, code and uncertainty definitions where available. Follow published dataset licenses and retain citations.

Build an evidence-coverage table for every adopted property, equation and benchmark. Preserve distinctions between direct measurements, inverse fits, digitized curves, secondary reporting and assumptions. Complete missing full-text access before adopting unseen coefficients. Use separate named material cases when study conditions are incompatible, and propagate consequential uncertainty into outputs.

This register does not validate the complete apparatus or the proposed 100 US gpm water delivery. It supplies traceable starting points for the next implementation and validation cycle.
